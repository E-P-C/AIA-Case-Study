AKP CORP Employee Handbook Version 2.1
Effective Date: 2025‑03‑10
Applicable to: Full‑time employees, technical contractors, site‑based consultants

## 1. General Code of Conduct
All staff shall act in the best interest of AKP Corp. Discrimination, harassment or disruptive behaviour inside office or on remote communication channels is strictly prohibited.
Employees must respect data classification levels: Public, Internal‑Use, Confidential, Restricted.
Restricted materials may only be accessed by explicitly granted role‑based permissions.

全体员工行为应当维护公司利益。严禁在办公环境以及远程沟通渠道发生歧视、骚扰等不当行为。
公司数据分为四级：公开、内部使用、机密、受限。受限资料仅允许被授予对应角色权限人员访问。

## 2. Working Hours & Leave Policy
Standard working hours: 9:00‑18:00, Monday‑Friday, one‑hour lunch break.
Core working window: 10:00‑16:00; staff shall be reachable during core hours.
Annual leave entitlement:
‑ 0‑2 years service: 10 days paid annual leave
‑ 2‑5 years service: 15 days paid annual leave
‑ >5 years service: 20 days paid annual leave

Sick leave: provide medical certificate for leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
Leave submission: all leave requests must be raised via internal HR portal.
Leaves of 3 calendar days or above require both direct manager and HR approval.
Contractor staff are NOT covered by this annual‑leave policy; refer to external service agreement.

工作核心时段10:00‑16:00，该时间段员工需要保持可联系。
病假连续超过2天需要提供医疗证明。所有假期在HR系统提交申请。3天及以上假期直属经理与HR双重审批。外包人员不适用本年假条款。

## 3. Confidentiality Obligation
All employees shall not copy, screenshot, forward or leak internal documents, source‑code, customer data to external parties via chat tools, email or public cloud drives.
Do NOT feed any AKP internal confidential content into public third‑party generative‑AI tools.
Confidential obligations survive after employment termination. Violation may result in disciplinary action including contract termination and potential legal consequences.

员工离职之后保密义务依然生效。禁止将公司内部机密内容投喂外部公有大模型。违规可导致纪律处分、解除劳动合同乃至法律追责。

## 4. IT & Device Policy
Company‑issued laptops must be used for handling confidential business data.
Personal devices shall not store Restricted‑level company information.
USB external storage is blocked by default on corporate devices to prevent data exfiltration.

处理机密业务数据必须使用公司配发设备。个人设备不得存储受限级别的公司信息。公司设备默认禁用外部USB存储，防止数据外泄。

## 5. Hybrid Remote Work Guideline
Hybrid remote work is allowed: maximum 2 remote working days per week.
Remote work must be pre‑approved by direct line manager.
When working remotely, employees must connect to corporate VPN for accessing internal knowledge‑base and backend services.
Public untrusted Wi‑Fi hotspots shall not be used for handling confidential business tasks.

混合办公每周最多2天远程，必须提前直属经理审批。远程办公访问内部知识库、后端服务必须连接公司VPN。不可以使用公共不可信Wi‑Fi处理机密业务。

## 6. Intellectual Property
All work products, source code, design documents created by employees within job scope belong to AKP Corporation.
Employees are not allowed to publish internal work output to personal blogs, GitHub or external platforms without formal written approval.

员工工作范围内产出的代码、设计文档知识产权归属AKP公司。未经书面许可，员工不得把内部工作成果发布到个人博客、GitHub等外部平台。
